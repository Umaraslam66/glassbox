The peak, day by day

The frozen peak measure — the share of the 300 arriving inside the busiest 15-minute window — for each of the twenty mornings. It starts pinned at the bridge's ceiling, jumps when the ring road is found, then falls as the peak genuinely spreads. The change measured from day 2 rather than day 1 is exploratory: it was not the pre-registered comparison, and it is reported here only as an annotation.
